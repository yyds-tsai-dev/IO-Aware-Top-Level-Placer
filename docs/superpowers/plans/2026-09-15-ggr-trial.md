## Known interface constraints

The installed OpenROAD `readSegments` initializes grid/nets and checks supplied routes, but its source does not call `updateEdgesUsage`. Importing segments alone is therefore insufficient evidence that resource arrays represent external routes. GGR native capacity and via-demand semantics also differ from OpenROAD. A common evaluator needs validated per-layer edge capacities, baseline obstruction demand, and unique per-net wire occupancy on the same physical grid. Guide rectangles must never substitute for chosen wires.

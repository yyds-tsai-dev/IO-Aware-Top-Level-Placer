# Literature search: IO-aware top-level placement (papers to borrow from)

Date: 2026-09-18. Produced by an agent during the 2026-09-18 brainstorming
session as a broad literature sweep to anchor the v2 redesign against prior
work; copied verbatim into the repository on 2026-09-19. Caveats the report
itself states: Semantic Scholar returned HTTP 429 on every call and DBLP
returned an XML parse error on every call, so both sources were dropped and
every row below came only from arXiv/Crossref/OpenAlex; `-y 2018-2026` was
passed but only Semantic Scholar honours a year filter, so older canonical
references also surface; "abstract unavailable" rows are Crossref-only
records where OpenAlex's follow-up lookup also hit HTTP 429; nothing below is
added from memory.

Method: `paper-search` CLI (~/.local/bin/paper-search), ~75 queries across topics 1–11 plus variants, sources `arxiv,crossref,openalex` (Semantic Scholar returned HTTP 429 on every call; DBLP returned an XML parse error on every call; both dropped). `-y 2018-2026` was passed but only Semantic Scholar honours it, so older canonical hits also surfaced. Every row below came back from the CLI with the DOI/URL shown; nothing is added from memory. "abstract unavailable" = the CLI returned no abstract (Crossref-only record; OpenAlex hit 429 during follow-up lookups). Entries are deduplicated across topics; a paper that fits two topics is listed once and cross-referenced.

Anchors already in hand (CLI-confirmed, not re-listed): GrandPlan (Xiong, Lu, Pan, Ren et al., 2026, DOI 10.1145/3764386.3779591).

## Top 10 to read first

1. **Lin et al., ICCAD 2025 — Hybrid Fence Region Constraints via Non-Newtonian Fluid Model** (10.1109/iccad66269.2025.11240878): borrow the hybrid-region multi-electrostatic formulation so fences of several types live in one density system instead of one field per partition.
2. **Mai et al., ICCAD 2024 — MORPH** (10.1145/3676536.3676745): borrow the default/fence/guide region taxonomy and robustness tricks, treating soft partition membership as a guide region in DREAMPlace.
3. **Xu et al., 2025 — Flora, feedthrough & placement-aware rectilinear floorplanner** (arXiv 2507.14914): borrow their explicit feed-through cost and the three-stage floorplan/placement coupling; it is the nearest published objective to ours.
4. **Hong et al., ICCCAS 2022 — Channel-based SoC feedthrough insertion** (10.1109/icccas55266.2022.9825309) together with **Sakariya et al., ISQED 2025** (10.1109/isqed65160.2025.11014329): borrow the industrial definition of what a feed-through costs (channel congestion, die area) to calibrate our feed-through penalty.
5. **Xu et al., 2025 — Piano, pin-assignment-aware floorplanner** (arXiv 2508.13161) plus **Yu, Censor, Luo, TCAD 2024** (10.1109/TCAD.2024.3408106): borrow boundary pin assignment inside the floorplanning objective and the projection-based handling of pin-capacity constraints.
6. **Huang et al., ICCAD 2023 — electrostatics fixed-outline floorplanning with rotation/sizing** (10.1109/iccad57390.2023.10323841) and **Chen et al., ICCAD 2024 — rectilinear soft modules** (10.1145/3676536.3676818): borrow the polygon-clipping density and rectilinear soft-block handling for the top-level partition shapes.
7. **Li et al., DAC 2025 — Differentiable net-moving and local congestion mitigation** (10.1109/dac63849.2025.11133117): borrow the differentiable net-bounding-box congestion term as the routability component of our gradient.
8. **Liao et al., TCAD 2023 — DREAMPlace 4.0 momentum-based net weighting** (10.1109/tcad.2023.3240132) with **GradNorm** (arXiv 1711.02257) and **PCGrad** (10.48550/arxiv.2001.06782): borrow momentum-smoothed weight updates plus gradient-magnitude normalisation / conflict projection to keep IO-crossing, feed-through, fence and wirelength terms balanced across GP rounds.
9. **Kahng, Varadarajan, Wang, TCAD 2024 — Hier-RTLMP** (10.1109/tcad.2023.3346284) with **Vidal-Obiols et al., DATE 2019 / TCAD 2021** (10.23919/date.2019.8714812): borrow hierarchy-tree clustering and dataflow-driven top-level structure as the source of partitions and IO pin seeds.
10. **Lin et al., ICCAD 2024 — InstantGR** (10.1145/3676536.3676787) with **Liang et al., ISPD 2024 contest** (10.1145/3626184.3639693): borrow the GPU GR-as-routability-estimator setup and the contest metrics for a scalable in-loop evaluator on 30M-cell designs.

Honourable mentions for specific mechanisms: Kahng & Reda "Placement feedback" (pseudo-pin/terminal-propagation ambiguity, 10.1145/996566.996670); Roy & Markov Steiner wirelength in placement (10.1109/tcad.2006.888260); Zhu et al. TCAS-II 2023 incremental physical-aware re-partitioning (10.1109/tcsii.2023.3318352); MedPart differentiable hypergraph partitioning (10.1145/3626184.3633319); Zhang & Sapatnekar AccFFT for the density solve (10.1109/ASP-DAC66049.2026.11420709).
